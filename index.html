What Stemline does

You import an audio file already on your device. Stemline separates it into drums, bass, vocals, and everything else, then gives each part its own volume fader. Pull the vocals down to sing over the track, or pull an instrument down and play that part yourself.

The separation runs entirely on your iPhone. Your audio is never uploaded, no account is required, and the app works with no connection.

Common questions

Where do songs come from?

You supply them. Stemline has no catalog and no store — it does not search, stream, or download anything, and it cannot open DRM-protected tracks such as Apple Music or Spotify downloads. Import files you own or otherwise have the right to use.

Why is the first split slow?

The machine learning model compiles itself for your specific iPhone the first time it runs. That happens once. Later splits are considerably faster.

A split failed or the app quit partway through

Separation is memory-hungry. Close other apps and try again, and try a shorter file to confirm. If it keeps failing, email the details above with the track length and your iPhone model.

I can still hear a little vocal in the backing

Expected. Separation is very good, not perfect — some bleed between parts is normal, and loud, dense mixes are the hardest. Stemline is built for practising and singing along, not for release-quality remixing.

A fader says a part is missing

Some songs genuinely have no bass or no vocal. Stemline shows those parts as empty rather than pretending there is something to fade.

Can I export or share the separated parts?

Separated parts are derivative works of the original recording. Keep them for your own practice rather than distributing them.

Privacy

Read the full privacy policy.

Stemline collects nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no account, and no network use by the app itself. Your audio and the stems made from it stay on your device.
